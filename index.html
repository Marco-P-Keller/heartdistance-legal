What the app does

Your live distance

The app shows the distance between you and one other person. It updates when either of you moves. You never see a map or a location history — only the number of kilometres between you.

Send a pulse

Tap once to send a small signal that says “I am thinking of you”. No words needed, and no conversation to keep going.

Your milestones

The app remembers your closest and your farthest distance, and how many pulses you have sent each other.

Countdown to your reunion

Add the date when you will see each other again. The app counts down the days for both of you.

Their weather

See the weather where the other person is, so you know if they need a jacket today.

Built for exactly two

There is no feed, no friend list and no followers. One connection, and nothing else to manage.

How to start

  1. Download the appInstall HeartDistance on your iPhone from the App Store. You do not need to create an account or type in an email address.
  2. Share your pairing codeThe app gives you a short code. Send it to the one person you want to connect with.
  3. Stay connectedOnce you are paired, both phones show the same distance, the same milestones and the same countdown.

Our privacy promise

  • No accounts, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to leak.
  • No ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • Only your latest location is stored — never a history, never on a map.
  • Your data lives in your own Apple iCloud (CloudKit). We run no servers and cannot read your content.
  • We never sell or share your data.

HeartDistance Plus

The app is free to use. HeartDistance Plus is an optional subscription that unlocks the extra features inside the app. Apple handles the payment, and we never receive your payment details. You can cancel it at any time in your iPhone settings.

Questions people ask

Can the other person see where I am?
No. They only see how far away you are. There is no map and no address.
Do I need an account?
No. You connect with a pairing code, and that is all.
Can I connect with more than one person?
No. HeartDistance is made for exactly two people.
How do I disconnect?
Choose “Untie the thread” in the settings. This removes your data from the shared record.
Is there an Android version?
Not at the moment. HeartDistance is an iPhone app and it uses Apple iCloud to sync.
