## 5. Description

*(4000 characters max. The English block below is 1843, the German one 1817.)*

```
HeartDistance is an app for two people who miss each other.

It does one thing: it shows you how far apart you are right now. When one of you moves, the number changes. There is no map and no location history — only the distance between you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• See your live distance
Open the app and see how many kilometres are between you and one other person.

• Send a pulse
Tap once to say "I am thinking of you". You do not need words, and there is no conversation to keep going.

• Keep your milestones
The app remembers your closest and your farthest distance, and how many pulses you have sent each other.

• Count down to your reunion
Add the date when you will see each other again. Both phones count down the same days.

• Check their weather
See the weather where the other person is, so you know how their day feels.

BUILT FOR EXACTLY TWO

There is no feed, no friend list and no followers. You connect with one person using a short pairing code, and that is the whole app. Nothing to manage, nothing to scroll.

PRIVACY YOU CAN CHECK

• No accounts. You never type in an email address or a password.
• No ads and no advertising SDKs.
• Only your latest location is stored, never a history.
• Your data lives in your own Apple iCloud. We run no servers of our own and cannot read your content.
• We never sell or share your data.

You can remove your data at any time with "Untie the thread" in the settings.

HEARTDISTANCE PLUS

The app is free to use. HeartDistance Plus is an optional subscription that unlocks the extra features in the app. Apple handles the payment, so we never receive your payment details, and you can cancel at any time in your iPhone settings.

Privacy policy: https://marco-p-keller.github.io/heartdistance-legal/privacy.html
Support: https://marco-p-keller.github.io/heartdistance-legal/support.html
```
